This is the first post on this site.

I write here for one reason: writing is how I figure out what I think. iOS, AI, CS, career — whatever I'm working through, this is where it lands.

Posts are short by default. Long when they need to be. Always English.

### What you'll find

- **iOS** — notes on Swift, SwiftUI, the parts of the platform I'm wrestling with.
- **AI** — what I'm learning, slowly, mostly from primary sources.
- **CS** — fundamentals I dodged in school.
- **Career** — reflections on the trajectory.
- **Notes** — diary-ish, lower stakes.

If any of that is useful to you, [the RSS feed is here](/rss.xml).
